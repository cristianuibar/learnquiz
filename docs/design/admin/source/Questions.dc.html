Report damaged pallets before put-away

Critical 15 questions · 1 rejected

  • A pallet arrives with a crushed corner and torn wrap. What do you do first?

    Rejected Critical Difficulty 2

    Multiple choice · Unreviewed · Checked automatically Oct 28 · The correct answer isn't in the source quote

    1. A
      Put it away and note the damage on the record Correct answer
    2. B
      Re-wrap the pallet before put-away Feedback: Re-wrapping hides the damage.
    3. C
      Refuse the whole delivery Feedback: Only the receiving lead refuses a delivery.
    4. D
      Move it to the returns area No feedback

    Explanation

    Recording the damage keeps the stock count accurate while the pallet is in storage.

Double beep: put the item back and re-scan

Critical 14 questions · 1 flagged

  • Your handheld beeps twice after you scan an item. What do you do?

    Flagged by learner Critical Difficulty 2

    Multiple choice · Approved Oct 30 · Dana Whitaker

    Reported by Jordan Reyes · Nov 2, 10:14

    Reason
    The answer looks incorrect
    Note
    “I re-scanned it and the beep stopped. That's how my lead showed me on day 2.”
    Attempt
    Chose “Re-scan the item barcode” · Sure
    1. A
      Put it back, then scan the bin and the item Correct answer
    2. B
      Re-scan the item barcode Feedback: Scanning it again repeats the mismatch.
    3. C
      Put it in the tote and report it at drop-off Feedback: The wrong item ships to the customer.
    4. D
      Restart the handheld No feedback

    Explanation

    Two beeps mean the item doesn't match the pick. Putting it back first keeps the wrong item out of the tote.

Key in a barcode only as a last resort

12 questions · 1 flagged

  • The item barcode won't scan. When can you key in the number by hand?

    Flagged by learner Difficulty 2

    Multiple choice · Approved Oct 30 · Dana Whitaker

    Reported by Amara Okafor · Nov 2, 16:05

    Reason
    It doesn't match how we do it here
    Note
    “On our floor only the team lead keys in a barcode. We send the label to the help desk.”
    Attempt
    Chose “Never: send it to the help desk” · Sure
    1. A
      After three failed scans at 10 to 30 cm Correct answer
    2. B
      Whenever it's faster than scanning Feedback: A keyed number skips the scanner's check.
    3. C
      Before scanning, if the label looks worn Feedback: Worn labels often still scan. Try first.
    4. D
      Never: send it to the help desk No feedback

    Explanation

    Keying in is the fallback when the scanner can't read the label. Three tries at the right distance come first.

Refund only after inspection

Critical 11 questions · 11 unreviewed

  • A return arrives with the outer box still sealed. When can the refund go out?

    Live Critical Difficulty 3

    Multiple choice · Unreviewed · Generated Nov 2 from Returns procedure

    1. A
      After someone inspects the item Correct answer
    2. B
      As soon as the return label scans Feedback: A label scan doesn't show what's inside.
    3. C
      When the box weight matches the shipment Feedback: A damaged item can weigh the same.
    4. D
      After the customer confirms by phone No feedback

    Explanation

    A refund goes out only after someone has seen the item. A sealed box can still hold a damaged item.
