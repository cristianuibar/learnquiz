Who we are

Learn Quiz is run by [LEGAL ENTITY], [ADDRESS]. In this policy, “we” means that company.

Learn Quiz turns a company’s own procedures into practice questions for new hires. This policy explains what we record when you use it, who can see it, and what it is used for.

What we record

When you practice, we record:

  • Your name, and your work email if your invite came by email.
  • Your answers to each question.
  • How sure you said you were on each answer: Sure, Unsure or Guessing.
  • How long each answer took.
  • Your progress toward your role’s benchmark.

For company admins, we also record account details, such as name and work email, and the documents they upload.

What we never record

  • No camera, sensors or health data.
  • No personality scores.
  • No ranking against coworkers.

Who sees it

You, and your company’s Learn Quiz admins.

Your manager sees the same progress page you see. Nothing on it is hidden from you.

What it is for

Training and development only. We use your answers to decide what you review next and to show when you have reached your role’s benchmark.

AI processing

Questions and explanations are generated from your company’s own documents by Meta models. Each explanation quotes the document it comes from.

Written answers are graded by a model. A person decides on anything that matters.

Push notifications

If you allow them, we send short practice questions as push notifications during working hours only. You can turn them off at any time in your settings.

Ignoring a notification is never penalized. The question waits for your next session.

How long we keep it

We keep your training records for [RETENTION PERIOD]. After that, we delete them.

Your choices

You can see your own progress page at any time. To ask for a copy of your data, or to have it deleted, contact your company’s Learn Quiz admin or write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

The local edition

If your company runs the local edition, your data stays on your company’s own server. We do not receive it. Your company is responsible for how it is stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to [CONTACT EMAIL] or by mail to [LEGAL ENTITY], [ADDRESS].
