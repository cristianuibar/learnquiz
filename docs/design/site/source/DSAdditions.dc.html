Never rules kept

The marketing site adds no exceptions to the system's rules.

RuleHow the site keeps it
No gradientsFlat fills only. Device frames are a solid bezel color.
No shadows on cardsCards, the desktop frame and phones use a 1px border or outline. shadow-raised appears only on things that float: the demo's result sheet and the video dialog.
Saffron is never text or linesSaffron appears only as a button fill and as the logo's dot. Chart lines use brand and ink-muted.
One saffron button per viewHero and close buttons are saffron. The header button stays quiet while either is on screen.
Feedback colors only in the answer sectionGreen and red appear only in section 5: the outcome icons, the demo's answer states and the result band. Critical markers stay in ink.
