QA findings only. No product code, tests, CI, workspace configuration, dependencies, or migrations were changed for this audit.

## Fixture provenance, checks, and limits

Ready content was deliberately inserted into **local D1**, using the columns and JSON shapes in `apps/web/src/db/schema.ts`, `apps/web/src/db/auth-schema.ts`, and the item/procedure schemas. It was not presented as successful model output. The fixtures included:

- Company topic `qa-company-topic` and personal topic `qa-personal`, units/concepts, readable source text, active questions, and ready/pending/generating/failed concept states.
- MCQ, multi-select, cloze, short, and order payloads; an MCQ with an image and descriptive alt text. Short answers were exercised on the personal topic, not used as company placement questions.
- Single-question session records with queue/position/answered/reinserted/reasons/unit-start data. Answering, grading failure, reporting, and completion then used the real UI/server entry points. The joined learner's placement was started and completed through the UI rather than seeded.
- A blank PDF, a corrupt PDF, a readable one-page PDF, and the exact size-boundary file. Readable diagram PNGs were stored only in local R2 using `--local`, under the media route's expected item keys.
- Local invitation/membership/proficiency fixtures; three prior low-scoring sessions and weak mastery used temporarily for Back to basics; a fresh procedure-generation claim for pending and a schema-valid stored graph for ready. Temporary Today history, assignment, and mastery changes were restored.

Generation coverage included curriculum build/rebuild, concept-question retry, placement-question generation and retry, short-answer grading failure, concept-diagram failure/retry, Unit procedure failure/retry/pending/ready, and the surrounding empty/loading states. `/start`'s second “Writing your first questions” phase cannot naturally follow a failed curriculum call here; its underlying question-generation failure was exercised with the seeded ready curriculum through `/play/loading`. Successful model drafting/self-check, voice transcription, and paid planner/image generation were not validated without credentials. Stored ready content tests rendering and interaction, not model quality.

Verification completed:

- Real browser actions, accessibility-tree inspection, screenshot matrices, document-width measurements, and targeted keyboard focus checks. No missing names were established for visible form controls; hidden upload/color inputs were not counted as label failures. This is not a screen-reader certification or a full contrast audit.
- Browser request inspection showed no HTTP 500 response in the collected list. Expected 401/403/422 responses occurred on authentication/authorization/validation paths.
