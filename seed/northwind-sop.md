# Northwind Logistics: Warehouse picker SOP

Version 1.0 · Training role: Warehouse picker

Northwind Logistics is a fictional warehouse that receives, stores, picks and
dispatches boxed household goods. This original procedure is for its demo
training programme. Work only on equipment and tasks for which a supervisor has
authorised and trained you. If a label, instruction or stock record disagrees
with what you see, stop that task and ask the shift lead to resolve it.

## 1. Start the shift safely

1. Put on the required high-visibility vest and protective footwear before
   entering the warehouse floor. Check your scanner and trolley for damage.
   Report faults and use a replacement; do not repair equipment yourself.
2. Find the marked pedestrian route, emergency exits and the shift lead's
   location. Keep exits, fire equipment and pedestrian routes clear.
3. **Critical — vehicle separation:** Stay inside marked pedestrian routes. At a
   vehicle crossing, stop and wait for the vehicle to stop and its operator to
   acknowledge you before crossing. Never walk under raised forks or a suspended
   load. A scanner instruction never gives you permission to enter a vehicle lane.
4. Inspect the work area for spills, damaged pallets and unstable stacks. Keep
   others away from a hazard and notify the shift lead. Do not climb racking or
   use a pallet as a step.
5. **Critical — safe handling:** Check the package's weight and handling label
   before lifting. If you cannot handle it safely, leave it in place and request
   trained help or an approved lifting aid. Do not guess a safe weight or attempt
   an unstable lift to keep an order moving.

## 2. Receive and put away stock

1. Match the delivery reference to the expected receipt before unloading into
   the marked receiving area. Keep newly arrived stock separate from available
   stock until the receipt has been checked.
2. Count the actual cartons and inspect them for damage. Compare the item code,
   quantity and any required batch reference with the delivery record. Record
   the actual count, including shortages and excess stock.
3. **Critical — hold discrepancies:** Put damaged goods, unreadable labels and
   mismatched deliveries in the designated hold area. Record the reason and tell
   the shift lead. Do not mark them as available or substitute an expected item
   code for the one on the carton.
4. Scan the receiving reference and the item's own label. Check the scanner's
   item description and unit of measure: one carton may contain several units.
   Enter the counted quantity in the displayed unit of measure.
5. For put-away, scan the destination location label and then the stock label.
   Confirm that the displayed destination matches the physical location. Place
   stock within the marked storage boundary and confirm only after it is there.
   If the destination is full or unsafe, request another location from the lead.

## 3. Pick and scan an order

1. Sign in with your own scanner account. Open the assigned order and scan the
   empty tote's label to link that tote to the order. Keep one order per tote.
2. Follow the displayed pick location using pedestrian routes. At the shelf,
   scan the physical location label before touching the stock.
3. Scan the label on the actual item you will pick. Compare the displayed item
   code, description, variant and unit of measure with the item and order.
4. **Critical — resolve a scan mismatch:** If the scanner reports the wrong
   location or item, stop the pick and notify the shift lead. Do not override the
   warning, scan a nearby item's label, or type the expected code to force a
   match. Resume only after the discrepancy has been resolved.
5. Count the requested units and inspect them for damage before placing them in
   the linked tote. Confirm the quantity actually placed in the tote. A request
   for three individual units is not a request for three cartons.
6. If stock is short or damaged, record the actual available quantity and the
   exception in the scanner. Leave the missing quantity unconfirmed and ask the
   lead for the next action. Do not substitute a similar product or borrow stock
   from another order's tote.
7. Keep heavy goods below lighter goods and keep labels visible. Do not exceed
   the trolley's marked capacity. Secure the tote before moving to the next pick.

## 4. Check and hand over a completed pick

1. Move the tote to the assigned checking station and scan its label. Compare
   the physical contents and quantities with the order shown on the scanner.
2. Record any discrepancy and keep the tote in the checking area while it is
   resolved. Do not confirm an order as complete with an outstanding shortage,
   extra item or scan warning.
3. When the check is complete, scan the assigned staging location, place the
   tote there and confirm the handover. Keep different orders separate and keep
   the staging aisle clear. Dispatch approval belongs to the authorised dispatch
   colleague; a completed pick alone does not authorise shipment.

## 5. Handle returns

1. Move a return to the marked returns area. Scan the return reference and
   compare the item's own label, quantity and condition with the return record.
   Record what arrived, including missing parts and damaged packaging.
2. Keep unidentified returns separate and ask the shift lead to identify them.
   Do not invent an order reference or change an item code to make a return fit.
3. **Critical — quarantine before release:** Place every return in the designated
   returns hold area until an authorised checker records its disposition. A
   sealed box or a customer's description is not approval to return it to
   available stock. Never pick an order from returns hold.
4. After the checker authorises restocking, follow the normal put-away scans for
   the approved quantity. Goods assigned to another disposition remain separate
   for the authorised colleague to handle. Do not dispose of them yourself.

## 6. End the shift and report exceptions

1. Leave each open tote at its recorded location and tell the next colleague
   which orders have unresolved exceptions. Record the item or order reference,
   observed problem, location and action already taken.
2. Return the trolley to its marked parking area, sign out of your scanner and
   put it on charge. Report faults, near misses and unresolved hazards to the
   shift lead before leaving.
